Spring arrived, and Jeomsun kept finding reasons to see the boy. Excuses came more easily than affection.

Three potatoes in a white cloth: a small gift of affection. He mistook it for a taunt about his poverty.

Jeomsun drew the potatoes back and pressed her lips together. Her hurt had nowhere to go.

Soon, even the roosters were drawn into their quarrel. Her wounded pride kept feeding his anger.

His family farmed her family’s land. Cradling his rooster, the boy swallowed the anger he could not afford.

Another rooster fight. This time, the boy struck Jeomsun’s bird. It did not get up again.

What if his family lost their home? Fear broke into tears. Only then did Jeomsun understand his pain.

Jeomsun reassured him that she would not tell. A gentle hand on his shoulder began to undo their misunderstanding.

Neither knew how to name the feeling. Among the yellow blossoms, spring arrived before the words.
