## English description — ready to paste

Before words, the heart blooms.

A gift of three potatoes becomes a misunderstanding. Hurt turns to mischief, until an encounter among yellow spring blossoms reveals the fear and tenderness beneath two young hearts.

Directed by YANG SUK YOO, *Spring Before Words* is an AI short film adapted from Kim Yu-jeong's *Dongbaekkot*. In place of character dialogue, a storyteller's narration, quiet expressions, and small gestures carry this story of awkward first affection.

## 감독 소개 / Director biography

유양석은 AI를 활용해 이야기를 영상으로 만드는 AI 영화감독이다. 《말보다 먼저 핀 봄》에서는 김유정의 「동백꽃」을 바탕으로, 생성형 영상과 이야기꾼의 내레이션을 결합해 두 인물의 오해와 화해를 표현한다. 이번 작품은 큰 사건보다 작은 표정과 손길에 담긴 감정을 따라간다.

YANG SUK YOO is an AI filmmaker who uses generative tools to bring stories to the screen. In *Spring Before Words*, an adaptation of Kim Yu-jeong's *Dongbaekkot*, Yoo combines AI-generated imagery with a storyteller's narration to explore misunderstanding and reconciliation. The film follows emotion through small expressions and gestures rather than spectacle.
